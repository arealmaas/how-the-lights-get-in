Picks, notes and debate verdicts are saved in this browser; “Copy link to my picks” carries picks and verdicts to another device or a friend, “Export notes” writes everything to a Markdown file, and “Export picks to calendar” adds them all to your calendar at once.

Music, comedy and film links were looked up by hand; where none was found the planner offers a search instead.
